Chicken Pesto Kabobs

Herby, flavourful and perfect for sharing

🍗 Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken breast, cut into cubes
  • 1/3 cup basil pesto
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into chunks
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, cut into chunks
  • 1 red onion, cut into chunks
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Wooden or metal skewers

👩‍🍳 How to Make It

  1. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Mix the pesto, olive oil, lemon juice and a little black pepper in a bowl.
  3. Add the chicken and toss to coat. Refrigerate for 20–30 minutes.
  4. Thread the chicken, bell peppers and onion onto the skewers.
  5. Cook on a preheated grill or grill pan, turning regularly, until the chicken is fully cooked and reaches 74°C (165°F) internally.
  6. Serve warm with a side salad or your favourite whole grains.
