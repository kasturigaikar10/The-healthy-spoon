Quinoa Fruit Salad

Fresh fruit with a wholesome twist

🍓 Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup uncooked quinoa
  • 1 cup strawberries, chopped
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1 kiwi, peeled and chopped
  • 1 cup diced pineapple
  • 1 orange, peeled and segmented
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • Fresh mint leaves (optional)

👩‍🍳 How to Make It

  1. Rinse the quinoa and cook it according to the package instructions.
  2. Allow the cooked quinoa to cool completely.
  3. Wash and prepare all the fruit.
  4. In a small bowl, mix the lime juice and honey or maple syrup.
  5. Combine the cooled quinoa and fruit in a large bowl.
  6. Pour over the dressing and gently toss everything together.
  7. Garnish with mint if desired. Serve chilled.
