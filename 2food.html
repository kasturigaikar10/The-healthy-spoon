2.Avocado pasta

Ingredients :
  1. 12 ounces spaghetti
  2. 2 ripe avocados, halved, seeded and peeled
  3. /2 cup fresh basil leaves
  4. 2 cloves garlic
  5. 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
  6. Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  7. 1/3 cup olive oil
  8. 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  9. 1/2 cup canned corn kernels, drained and rinsed

Recipe :

1. In a large pot of boiling salted water, cook pasta according to package instructions; drain well.
2. To make the avocado sauce, combine avocados, basil, garlic and lemon juice in the bowl of a food processor; season with salt and pepper, to taste. With the motor running, add olive oil in a slow stream until emulsified; set aside.
3. In a large bowl, combine pasta, avocado sauce, cherry tomatoes and corn.
4. Serve immediately.
