Avocado Pasta

Creamy, colourful and full of flavour

🥑 Ingredients

  • 12 ounces spaghetti
  • 2 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup canned corn, drained and rinsed

👩‍🍳 How to Make It

  1. Cook spaghetti in boiling salted water according to the packet instructions. Drain and reserve a little pasta water.
  2. Blend avocado, basil, garlic and lemon juice until smooth.
  3. Slowly add olive oil while blending. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Mix the sauce with the cooked pasta, cherry tomatoes and corn. Add a little pasta water if needed.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!
