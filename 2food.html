Avocado Pasta

Creamy, fresh and delicious!

🥑 Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta of your choice
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh basil leaves
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Cherry tomatoes, halved (optional)
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

👩‍🍳 How to Make It

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions. Save a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Blend the avocado, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil and basil until creamy.
  3. Add a little pasta water to loosen the sauce if needed.
  4. Toss the cooked pasta with the avocado sauce.
  5. Season with salt and black pepper. Add tomatoes or Parmesan if desired, then serve.
