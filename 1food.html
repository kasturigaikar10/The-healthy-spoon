Greek Yogurt Chicken Salad Sandwich

A fresh and flavourful sandwich idea

🥑 Ingredients

  • 2 cups leftover rotisserie chicken
  • 1/2 cup diced red onion
  • 1/2 cup diced apple
  • 1/2 cup grapes, halved
  • 1/4 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/4 cup slivered almonds
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 8 slices bread
  • 4 lettuce leaves

👩‍🍳 How to Make It

  1. Combine chicken, onion, apple, grapes, cranberries and almonds in a bowl.
  2. Add Greek yogurt, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Mix well.
  3. Place lettuce and the chicken mixture on slices of bread.
  4. Cover with the remaining bread, slice and serve.
