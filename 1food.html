1.Greek yogurt chicken salad sandwich

Ingredients :
  1. 2 cups leftover rotisserie chicken
  2. 1/2 cup diced red onion
  3. /2 cup diced apple
  4. 1/2 cup grapes, halved
  5. 1/4 cup dried cranberries
  6. 1/4 cup slivered almonds
  7. /2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  8. 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice, or more, to taste
  9. 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  10. Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  11. 8 slices bread
  12. 4 leaves Boston bibb lettuce
    13.

Recipe :

1.In a large bowl, combine chicken, red onion, apple, grapes, dried cranberries, sliced almonds, Greek yogurt, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt and pepper, to taste.
2.Serve sandwiches on bread with chicken mixture and lettuce.
