        <b><h2>Ingredients :</h2></b>
        <ol><big>
        <li> 2 cups cooked quinoa</li>
        <li> 1 mango, peeled and diced</li>
        <li> 1 cup strawberries, quartered</li>
        <li> 1/2 cup blueberries</li>
        <li> 2 tablespoons pine nuts</li>
        <li> Chopped mint leaves, for garnish</li>
            <br>
         <li> FOR THE LEMON VINAIGRETTE</li>
         <li> 1/4 cup olive oil</li>
         <li> 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar</li>
         <li> Zest of 1 lemon</li>
         <li> 3 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice</li>
         <li> 1 tablespoon sugar</li>
         </big>
         </ol>
         <b><h2>Recipe :</h2></b>
         <p><big> 1. To make the vinaigrette, whisk together olive oil, apple cider vinegar, lemon zest and juice, and sugar in a small bowl; set aside.<br>
            2. In a large bowl, combine quinoa, mango, strawberries, blueberries and pine nuts. Stir in lemon vinaigrette.<br>
           3.  Serve immediately, garnished with mint leaves. </big></p>
