        <b><h1><center>5.Chicken Pesto Kabobs</center> </h1></b>   
        
    </head>
    <body>
        <img src="5food.png">
        <b><h2>Ingredients :</h2></b>
        <ol><big>
           <li>  1 cup pesto, homemade or store-bought</li>
           <li>  1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1 1/4-inch cubes</li>
           <li>  2 pints cherry tomatoes</li>
           <li>  Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste</li>
           <li>  2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley leaves</li>
           </big>
        </ol>
        
        <b><h2>Recipe :</h2></b>
        <p> <big>1. In a gallon size Ziploc bag or large bowl, combine chicken and pesto; marinate for at least 30 minutes to overnight, turning the bag occasionally. Drain chicken from the pesto.<br>
            2.Thread chicken and cherry tomatoes onto skewers; season with salt and pepper, to taste.<br>
            3.Preheat grill to medium high heat.<br>
            4. Add skewers to grill, and cook, turning occasionally, until the chicken is completely cooked through, reaching an internal temperature of 165 degrees F, about 10-12 minutes.<br>
            5.Serve immediately, garnished with parsley, if desired.</big></p>
