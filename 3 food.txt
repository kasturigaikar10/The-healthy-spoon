        <b><h2>Ingredients :</h2></b>
        <ol><big>
         <li>3 cups cooked quinoa </li>
         <li>  1 (4-ounce) can green chiles</li>
         <li>  1 cup corn kernels</li>
         <li>  1/2 cup canned black beans, drained and rinsed</li>
         <li>  1/2 cup petite diced tomatoes</li>
         <li>  1/2 cup shredded pepper jack cheese</li>
         <li>  1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese</li>
         <li>  3 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro leaves</li>
         <li>  1 teaspoon cumin</li>
         <li>  1 teaspoon garlic powder</li>
         <li>  1/2 teaspoon onion powder</li>
         <li>  1/2 teaspoon chili powder, or more to taste</li>
         <li>  Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste</li>
         <li>  6 bell peppers, tops cut, stemmed and seeded</li>
         </big></ol>
         <b><h2>Recipe :</h2></b>
         <p> <big>1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Line a 9×13 baking dish with parchment paper.<br>
            2.In a large bowl, combine quinoa, green chiles, corn, beans, tomatoes, cheeses, cilantro, cumin, garlic, onion and chili powder, salt and pepper, to taste.
             Spoon the filling into each bell pepper cavity.<br>3. Place on prepared baking dish, cavity side up, and bake until the peppers are tender and the filling is heated through, about 25-30 minutes.<br>
            4.Serve immediately.
           </big> </p>
