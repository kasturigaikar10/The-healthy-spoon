Quinoa Stuffed Bell Peppers

A colourful and wholesome meal

🫑 Ingredients

  • 4 bell peppers
  • 1 cup uncooked quinoa
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (optional)
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

👩‍🍳 How to Make It

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Rinse the quinoa and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. Cut the tops off the bell peppers and remove the seeds.
  4. Heat olive oil in a pan. Cook the onion and garlic until softened.
  5. Mix in the cooked quinoa, black beans, corn, tomatoes, cumin, salt and pepper.
  6. Fill each pepper with the quinoa mixture and place in a baking dish.
  7. Cover and bake for about 30–35 minutes, until the peppers soften. Add cheese near the end if desired.
  8. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve warm.
